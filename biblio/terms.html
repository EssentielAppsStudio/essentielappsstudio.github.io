Biblio lets you manage a personal library: add books (barcode scan, ISBN lookup or manual entry), track your reading, organise your books into genres, series and collections, record loans and borrowings, import and export a CSV catalogue and back up your data. It works without an account; your data stays on your phone (see the privacy policy).
