Biblio: privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Version française

In short: your library stays on your phone. The app has no account and no server: it sends me no data and I have no access to anything you record. No ads, no tracking, no usage statistics.

1. Publisher

Biblio is published by Essentiel Apps Studio, an independent publisher based in France.
Contact: essentielapps.studio@gmail.com

2. Your library data

The app stores the data you enter:

  • your books: title, authors, ISBN, publisher, genres, series, collections, rating, review, private notes…;
  • your reading: status, start and finish dates, reading history;
  • your loans and borrowings, including the name of the people you lend a book to or borrow one from;
  • covers: downloaded, picked from your gallery or photographed (resized by the app).

This data is stored only on your phone, in the app’s private storage. I never have access to it. Names recorded in loans stay on your phone, under your responsibility.

3. Services contacted by the app

Your phone contacts these services directly. As with any Internet connection, they receive the device’s IP address. I have no access to any of these exchanges.

  • Bibliothèque nationale de France and Open Library: when you scan or type an ISBN, the app queries the general catalogue of the BnF (French national library) and Open Library (a service of the non-profit Internet Archive), then downloads the cover from Open Library. What is sent: the ISBN, your phone’s language, and the app’s name with its version and the studio’s contact address. Nothing else about you or your library. See the BnF privacy policy and the Internet Archive terms.
  • Google Play services barcode scanner: the image is analysed on the phone and is not sent to Google. Google receives technical statistics about how the scanner works (phone model, versions, performance, errors) and may send it updates (ML Kit terms). The app itself has no access to the camera.
  • Phone camera: to photograph a cover, the app opens your phone’s camera app. The photo is saved in the app and sent nowhere.
  • Google Play: for the in-app purchase (section 4).

4. Free trial and purchase

The app is a free download on Google Play and can be used freely during a 14-day free trial. To keep adding and editing books afterwards, a one-time purchase is offered in the app, through Google Play. Without buying, the app stays in read-only mode: you can still search, export and back up your books, and your data is kept.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google, under its own rules: I never receive your bank details.
  • The app checks with Google Play, from the phone, that the purchase was made, and keeps that answer on the phone together with the trial start date. No server of the publisher is involved.
  • I only receive the order information Google provides to developers (for example for a refund).

5. What the app does not do

  • No account and no publisher server: no data is sent to me.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics, audience measurement or crash reporting tools.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, files or photos (except the one you pick for a cover).

6. Permissions

  • Internet access: to look up book information, download covers and check the purchase.
  • Google Play billing: to buy the full version.

7. Backups

  • Biblio backup and CSV export: from Settings, you can save a full backup (.bibliotheque file, covers included) or export your catalogue as CSV. These files are saved where you choose and are sent nowhere by the app.
  • Android automatic backup: if turned on for your phone, Android may back up the app’s database and settings to your Google account, so you can find them on a new phone. Covers are not included. This backup is managed by Google and can be turned off in your phone’s settings.

8. Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as you keep it. You can delete a book at any time. To erase everything, uninstall the app or clear its data in the phone’s settings, then delete any backup files you saved.

9. Your rights

As no data is kept on a publisher server, I hold no information to access, correct or delete: you keep full control of your data (section 8). Exchanges with the BnF, Open Library and Google are governed by their own rules. For any question, write to the address in section 1. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

10. Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children and collects no data about them.

11. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. On each visit, GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses, among other things, for security purposes, as described in its privacy statement.

12. Changes

This policy may change along with the app. The date at the top of the page shows the latest update.
